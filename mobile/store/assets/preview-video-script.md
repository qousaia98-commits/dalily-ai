# App Preview Video — preparation script (≈30s)

1. **0–3s** — Dalily splash / brand mark on deep green.
2. **3–8s** — Customer home: search nearby trusted help.
3. **8–14s** — Provider profile + book flow.
4. **14–20s** — Provider jobs list → navigate / complete.
5. **20–26s** — Business Assistant briefing card.
6. **26–30s** — End card: “Dalily — book with confidence” + App Store / Play badges.

## Production notes

- Record on iPhone 15 Pro and Pixel 8 at 60fps.
- Export 1080×1920 (phone) and 1200×628 (feature teaser).
- No experimental UI; use production builds only.
- Subtitles: EN + AR burned-in optional.
